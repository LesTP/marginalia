How to read a page

  • The poem sits beside its notes; opening a note highlights the lines it discusses.
  • Focus filters the notes by subject, so you can follow one thread at a time.
  • Notes open one at a time, and each cites short references that link to the full records under Sources.
  • Some poems offer more than one text — Donne appears in both its 1633 spelling and a modernized reading text.

Poems are in the public domain (Donne, 1633; Yeats, 1899). Scholarly notes paraphrase cited sources and link to each poem's Sources.
